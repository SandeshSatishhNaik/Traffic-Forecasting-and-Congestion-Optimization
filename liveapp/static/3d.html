How to read this

  1. Floor: the 21 road stretches, coloured by the speed recorded now. Dots are traffic: they crawl where the road is jammed.
  2. Walls rise above each road. Higher = further ahead: +15, +30, +60 min, forecast by the graph neural network.
  3. A wall that turns red as it rises means congestion is coming. A wall that turns green means it will clear.
  4. Rings on the floor: red = congested now, amber = early warning (fine now, forecast to congest).
  5. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover or click a road for details.

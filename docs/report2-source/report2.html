1. PROJECT CONTEXT

1.1 Background

Bengaluru's Outer Ring Road (ORR) carries a large share of the city's commuter and technology-park traffic. Speeds on it change within minutes. In our readings, weekday speed falls to about 60% of normal speed around 18:00 and to about 70% around 09:00 to 10:00, while on weekends and holidays it stays above 85% of normal all day. Navigation applications show the speed at this moment. They do not tell a commuter or a traffic manager what the speed will be in 15, 30 or 60 minutes, which is when a decision can still change the outcome. Roads also affect each other: a queue on one stretch spreads to the stretches that feed it. This is why the project treats the corridor as a connected road graph and not as separate roads.

1.2 Recap of Phase 1

Phase 1 scoped the problem as forecasting speed on connected road stretches 15, 30 and 60 minutes ahead, and turning the forecasts into congestion alerts and route recommendations. The literature on traffic forecasting was surveyed (statistical, recurrent, graph-convolutional and attention-based models), and an automatic collector for the ORR corridor was started on 27 September 2026.

1.3 What Phase 2 adds

  • Data. 19,093 valid speed readings on 21 connected road stretches, from 30 September to 1 November 2026 (33 days), with weather. Collection is still running; the models in this report use these first 33 days.
  • Road graph. The 21 stretches are linked by flow links and junction crossings, with OpenStreetMap features (lanes, speed limit, signals, intersections, schools, hospitals, offices, bus stops, metro) attached to each stretch.
  • Models. Eleven model versions compared on identical rows: four baselines (Persistence, Historical Average, Gradient Boosting on its own history, Gradient Boosting on all roads), three versions of a two-layer Graph Convolutional Network (GCN: no graph, road graph, road graph plus learned graph), Graph WaveNet, STID, and two versions of STID pretrained on the METR-LA benchmark.
  • Decisions. Congestion alerts, trip-time estimates and a time-dependent route planner built on the forecasts of the GCN with the road graph.
  • Visualisation. An offline replay dashboard that shows the forecast next to what actually happened.

1.4 Scope and constraints

  • Study area. The Outer Ring Road from Silk Board towards KR Puram in both directions (17 stretches) plus four feeder-road stretches (Hosur Road, Old Airport Road, Varthur Road).
  • Free data tiers. The TomTom free tier allows 20,000 flow requests a month. The collection schedule uses 596 a day, which is 18,476 in a 31-day month.
  • Data licence. The data provider's terms do not allow publishing or storing the readings publicly. Raw readings are therefore kept out of the shared results; only code and aggregate results are shared. A permission request to the provider is drafted.
  • Compute. Models were trained on a four-core CPU without a GPU. The full Graph WaveNet check on METR-LA needs a GPU and is pending.
  • Evaluation. Chronological split by day: training before 18 October, validation 18 to 24 October, test 25 to 31 October 2026. One test week and one corridor, so the results are indicative and not general.

1.6 Key terms

TermMeaning
Road stretchOne TomTom road segment, the unit that is measured and forecast (21 in this project).
Reference speedA fixed normal speed for each road: the 95th percentile of its night-time speeds in the training period. Speeds are divided by it so slow and fast roads can be compared.
HorizonHow far ahead the forecast looks: 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
Road graphThe stretches as nodes and the links between them (flow links and junction crossings) as edges.
GCNGraph Convolutional Network: a neural network layer that mixes each road with its neighbours in the road graph.
AblationRemoving one part (here, the graph) from an otherwise identical model to measure what that part is worth.
MAE, RMSE, MAPEAverage absolute error in km/h; the same with large errors weighted more; error as a percentage of the real speed. Lower is better.
SeedOne training run with a particular random start. Results are reported as the mean of 5 seeds and their spread.

10.4 Models and evaluation code

IDArtifactLocationWhat it is and the evidence
C1Data preparationmodeling/prepare.pyLoads readings, builds the 15-minute grid, the fixed reference speed, calendar flags and the road adjacency matrix.
C2Baselinesmodeling/baselines.pyPersistence (last value), Historical Average and Gradient Boosting (scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor).
C3Graph Convolutional Networkmodeling/stgnn.pyTwo-layer GCN predicting all 21 stretches together, in three variants that differ only in the graph: none, road graph, road graph plus learned graph.
C4Evaluationmodeling/evaluate.pyTrains and scores all of the above on identical rows, 5 seeds each, at 15, 30 and 60 minutes.
C5Hyper-parameter searchmodeling/tune.pyRandom search over 30 settings with 3 seeds each, using only data before the test week. Chosen: hidden size 64, embedding 4, dropout 0.2, learning rate 0.003, weight decay 0, batch 32, 3 lags.
C6Congestion alertsmodeling/alerts.pyRaises an alert when the forecast speed is below 0.6 or 0.75 of the reference speed, and scores it against an alert from the current speed.
C7Trip time and routingmodeling/routing.pyTime-dependent Dijkstra over the stretch graph (cost of a stretch = length divided by speed when the vehicle enters it) and trip-time estimates scored against the real trip.
C8Dashboard generatormodeling/demo_dashboard.pyBuilds one offline HTML page that replays 160 moments of the test week. The page embeds readings, so it is not stored in the repository.
C9Graph WaveNet and STIDmodeling/refmodels.py, refbengaluru.py, evaluate_ref.pyOur re-implementations of the two published models, trained and scored exactly like the GCN (same rows, 5 seeds).
C10METR-LA benchmarkmodeling/metrla.pyStandard protocol (12 steps in, 12 out, 70/10/20 split, masked metrics) for STID and Graph WaveNet.
C11Pretrainingmodeling/pretrain.pyPretrains STID on METR-LA, then fine-tunes on Bengaluru (all weights, or main layers frozen).
